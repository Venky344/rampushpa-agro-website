Corporate Profile About Us

Who We Are

Empowering Agriculture, Delivering Excellence

Founded in 2017, Rampushpa Agro Processing has rapidly emerged as Maharashtra's premier grain cleaning and processing powerhouse.

Based in the industrial zone of Beed, we operate a sophisticated double-unit facility designed for premium-grade optical sorting and mechanical purification of grains. We connect regional farming communities to bulk buyers across India by supplying certified impurity-free produce.

2017 - Foundation

Established primary cleaning operations with local sorting lines.

2021 - Technological Upgrade

Installed modern optical Color Sortex sorters to achieve 99.9% purity levels.

🎯 Our Mission

To secure agricultural value for farmers through state-of-the-art cleaning, ensuring premium quality grains reach households and FMCG distributors.

👁️ Our Vision

To set national benchmarks in agricultural sorting technology, becoming the first-choice supply partner for grain wholesalers across Western India.

🛡️ Core Values

Uncompromising purity standards, total transparency with farmers, environmental stewardship, and continuous machinery updates.

Farm to Market Journey

Standard Operating Procedure for 99.9% Purity

01

Farmer Sourcing

Ethical procurement directly from certified local crop growers in Beed and surrounding Maharashtra regions.

02

Mechanical Pre-Cleaning

Sieving and air aspiration channels extract straw, husks, stones, mud clumps, and dust impurities.

03

Grading & Sizing

Sifters segregate grains into uniform sizes, screening out shriveled, cracked, or deformed pieces.

04

Optical Color Sortex Sorter

Advanced optical CCD sorting inspects every seed, firing pressurized air jets to isolate off-color defects.

05

Packing & Dispatch

Hygienic weighing and manual bagging stations pack grains into secure bags, ready for dispatch to national trade hubs.

Process Rigor Quality Assurance

Guaranteed Standards

Zero-Defect Processing Philosophy

🔍

1. Intake Inspection

Incoming crop batches are moisture-tested. Grains exceeding threshold moisture are rejected to avoid rot.

💨

2. Double Destoning

Heavy duty pressure destoners filter out glass, stones, mud balls, and metal pieces prior to optical sorting.

📦

3. Secure Packing & Dispatch

Hygienic bagging and sealing stations pack grains securely into dust-proof premium woven sacks.

Innovation & Machinery Technology

State-of-the-Art Sorters

Advanced Optical Processing Units

We integrate advanced agricultural processing machinery to automate flow lines, eliminating slow, inaccurate manual hand-picking.

📸

CCD Camera Sorters
