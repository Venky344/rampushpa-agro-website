Introduction

Pioneering Grain Purity Standards in Maharashtra

Established in 2017 in Beed, Rampushpa Agro Processing operates state-of-the-art cleaning, sorting, and grading units. We specialize in processing high-quality local crops, bridging the gap between farming communities and wholesale buyers nationwide.

Through closed-loop pneumatic circuits, high-speed destoners, and optical sorting systems, we ensure zero sand, dust, or organic contaminants, achieving a certified 99.9% product purity standard.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

What began in 2017 as a regional sifting facility quickly expanded through forward-thinking technology integration. By setting up advanced machinery and robust logistics routes, Rampushpa became the primary supply partner for wholesale trade desks across Maharashtra.

Today, we process thousands of tons of grains annually, representing a key pillar in the region's agricultural value chain.

Workflow Journey

The Journey of a Grain

From raw crop field collection to high-trust commercial distribution desks.

Step 01

Raw Sourcing

Acquired directly from high-yield farming belts in Maharashtra. Density checks determine grain grade suitability.

Step 02

Aspiration Cleaning

Passed through vibro-cleaners and industrial destoners to drop heavy stones and lift light organic dust.

Step 03

Precision Grading

Screen separators sort grains by size, separating shrivelled or broken elements to ensure even product sizing.

Step 04

Optical Color Sorting

High-definition lenses separate damaged or color-blemished grains, achieving 99.9% pure, wholesome grains.

Step 05

Pneumatic Packaging

Weighed scales fill custom moisture-proof bags which are double-stitched automatically to ensure durable transit.
